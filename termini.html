Budget Famiglia - Terms of use

Scope

Budget Famiglia is a private tool for personal, non-commercial use by the members of a single family. It is not a service offered to third parties, and nobody outside the family may use it.

Use of account data

  • The application accesses only the accounts and cards of the people who have personally authorised it, and only for reading (balances and transactions). It cannot initiate payments or change anything at the bank.
  • The data is used solely for the family's own household budget.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty. It does not give financial advice. The authoritative record of any account is the bank's own statement.

Withdrawing consent

Each account holder can withdraw consent at any time from the bank's own app or website.
