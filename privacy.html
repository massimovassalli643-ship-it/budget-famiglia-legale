Budget Famiglia - Privacy notice

What this application is

Budget Famiglia is a private, non-commercial tool used by a single family to keep its own household budget. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it reads

With the explicit consent of each account holder, given through the bank's own strong customer authentication via Enable Banking, the application reads the balances and transactions of the payment accounts and cards of that holder. No other data is requested.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to prepare the family's own household budget.
  • It is stored only on the devices of the account holders. It is not published, sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.
  • Access credentials are kept on the owner's computer and are never shared.

Consent and your rights

Consent is given by each account holder personally and expires automatically after the period set by the bank. It can be withdrawn at any time from the bank's own app or website, after which the application can no longer read the data. Data already stored can be deleted on request by the owner of the application.

Budget Famiglia - Informativa sulla privacy

Budget Famiglia è uno strumento privato e senza scopo commerciale, usato da una sola famiglia per il proprio bilancio domestico. Con il consenso esplicito di ciascun titolare, dato con l'autenticazione forte della banca tramite Enable Banking, legge saldi e movimenti dei conti e delle carte di quel titolare. I dati servono solo al bilancio familiare e restano solo sui dispositivi dei titolari: non sono pubblicati, venduti né comunicati a terzi. Il consenso scade da solo e può essere revocato in qualsiasi momento dall'app o dal sito della banca.
